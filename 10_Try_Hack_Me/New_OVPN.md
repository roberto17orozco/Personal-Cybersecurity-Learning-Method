# Get a new OVPN
If for some reason your Try Hack Me **OpenVPN** file doesn't work when you type ```sudo openvpn <ovpn file name>``` it may be expired or broken, so you would have to **get another one.**

1. Navigate to [TryHackMe](tryhackme.com)
2. Clic on your profile picture.
3. Manage account.
4. VM and VPN Settings.
5. `Download Regular Configuration File`.

---
6. Remove your **old .ovpn file**
7. Move the **new .ovpn** file to the *desired directory.*
8. ```sudo openvpn <ovpn file name>```.
.
.
.

## Reason for this problem
OpenVPN files may not work for different reasons:
1. File may expire after 30 days, specially on a free account.
2. Probable change of server or IP linked to my file.
